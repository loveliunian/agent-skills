## 项目影响扫描

将扫描命令、范围、命中文件和判断写入 `.devflow/{change-id}/project-scan.txt`。以下十项每项必须为 `HIT|MISS|NA`：

```text
SCAN_DB=
SCAN_DOMAIN=
SCAN_API=
SCAN_CLIENT=
SCAN_CONFIG=
SCAN_TEST=
SCAN_PERMISSION=
SCAN_WORKFLOW=
SCAN_CROSS_SERVICE=
SCAN_HISTORY_DATA=
```

权限、流程、跨服务或历史数据命中时必须 FULL。

## 机器契约

写入 `.devflow/{change-id}/small-change.env`：

```text
CHANGE_KIND=ui-behavior
CHANGE_SUBJECT={change-subject}
LOGICAL_CHANGE_COUNT=1
TARGET=merge-ready
SURFACES=ui
PROJECT_SCAN_EVIDENCE=.devflow/{change-id}/project-scan.txt
CHANGE_REPORT=docs/小需求变更/{change-id}-小需求变更.md
AFFECTED_PATHS={comma-separated-paths}
BREAKING_API=0
TYPE_OR_NULLABILITY_BREAKING=0
PERMISSION_CHANGE=0
STATE_MACHINE_CHANGE=0
CROSS_SERVICE_CHANGE=0
NEW_TABLE_OR_SERVICE=0
LARGE_BACKFILL=0
MIGRATION_REQUIRED=0
DIALECTS=
VERIFY_CMD={focused-command}
MIGRATION_VERIFY_CMD=
DEPLOY_RECEIPT_PATH=
MONITOR_RECEIPT_PATH=
DECISION=MICRO
DECISION_REASON={project-evidence-based-reason}
```
